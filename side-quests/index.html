Side Quest 01

RabbidBracket

Announced · Releasing soon

Run the tournament. Not the paperwork.

RabbidBracket is a tournament facilitator for steel-tip darts, built for venues, leagues, and charity shoots. Configure the format, enter the players, and it handles the draw, the boards, the standings, the payouts, and the big screen — start to champion.

It exists because running a tournament from a clipboard and a whiteboard is a second job on top of hosting one. Brackets get redrawn by hand, boards get double-booked, and the payout math gets settled in the parking lot. RabbidBracket takes that whole job.

  • Automatic bracket generation with random seeding and byes
  • Live board scheduling that won't double-book a board
  • Payouts that reconcile to the cent
  • Big-screen display for boards, brackets, and standings
  • Mass score entry for paper-run group stages
  • Fix a wrong score and watch the bracket cascade correctly
  • Outcome-based scoring: legs won, not every dart thrown
  • Event history and resumable tournaments

Side Quest 02

RabbidMARC

Announced · In development

Read the reports. Not the XML.

RabbidMARC is a DMARC analytics platform for Microsoft 365 environments. It reads the aggregate reports that mailbox providers send back about your domains, and shows you who is sending mail as you and whether it is passing.

It exists because those reports arrive as zipped XML from dozens of receivers, and nobody reads them by hand. Getting to p=reject safely means finding every legitimate sender first, and RabbidMARC does that legwork. It is in use internally at RabbidLabs today, and we are exploring making it available more broadly.

  • Read-only collection from Microsoft 365 mailboxes through Microsoft Graph
  • Multiple domains, subdomains, and mailboxes in one place
  • Sender identification that turns raw IPs into named services
  • Live DNS checks of your DMARC, SPF, and DKIM posture
  • A guided path from p=none to p=reject
  • Alerts for new unauthenticated senders and compliance drops
  • Compliance over time, with DKIM and SPF alignment breakdowns
  • Aggregate, forensic, and TLS-RPT report support
